How this list is ranked

  • One list for TV and streaming: a show that is on both counts once.
  • This week: BARC weekly TV ratings are still paused and FMN has not posted its Week 41 chart yet, so the TV places use FMN's Week 40 chart. I couldn't find Ormax's weekly streaming chart for 5–11 Oct, so streaming places use Netflix figures reported by Chrome DM COTT and Koimoi.
