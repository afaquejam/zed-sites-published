How this list is ranked
  • Popularity: UK and European weekly sales charts, the PlayStation Store's monthly top downloads, Circana's US chart and Famitsu's Japan chart.
  • Ratings: Metacritic (the PlayStation version's score where there is one) and OpenCritic.
  • What counts: games on PS5 or PS4, including multiplatform games. Long-running games such as Fortnite or EA Sports FC take at most 3 places, so new releases have room.
  • This week: The PlayStation Store and Circana US charts for October aren't out yet, so those figures are still from September. The UK boxed chart is for the week to 24 Oct and the Famitsu Japan chart is for 19–25 Oct.
  • NEW: with a previous week to compare, NEW marks a title new to the list; on a list's first week, a title released in the last 3 weeks.
